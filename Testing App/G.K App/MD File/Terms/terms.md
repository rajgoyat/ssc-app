# Mugal Terms
Subadar → Province
Mir Bakshi → Army
Diwan → Finance
Mir Saman → Royal household
Chief Qazi → Judiciary

## Literary references (like Pattinappalai) mention customs officials collecting ulgu.
Other related revenue terms from Sangam literature include:
Karai / Irai → Land tax
Iravu → Forced gifts or extra demands
Irai → Tribute from feudatories
Ulgu ->  customs & Duties
Uzhavar → actual cultivators/ploughmen
Vellalar → important agricultural/landholding class, jo land ownership aur cultivation se associated thi.
War Drum → Murasu 🥁
Murasu ek important royal/war drum tha. War ke announcement, army mobilization aur royal authority se iska connection tha.

Important groups yaad rakho:

Arasar → Kings/ruling class
Uzhavar → Cultivators/farmers
Ayar → Pastoralists/cowherds
Vanigar → Merchants/traders
Panar → Bards/singers
Paraiyar → Drummers and other occupational roles in early Tamil society
Brahmanas/Antanar → Ritual and learned group; Vedic practices ka bhi reference milta hai
Warriors → warfare aur heroism se associated; heroic death par Nadukal erect kiya ja sakta tha

Sangam society ko Tinai system se bhi samjha jata hai:

Kurinji → hills → hunting
Mullai → pastoral → Ayar
Marutam → fertile plains → Uzhavar/agriculture
Neital → coast → fishing & maritime trade
Palai → dry region → travel/warfare
Banner/Flag → Kodi
Rulers apne dynastic symbols/emblems ko banners par use karte the.

Kalavu = premarital/secret love
Yani shaadi se pehle ka love relationship, secret meetings, attraction, lovers ka milna etc.

Karpu = married/chaste love
Yani shaadi ke baad husband-wife relationship, fidelity, domestic life aur marital love.
